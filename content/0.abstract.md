As autonomous agents increasingly write to shared RDF knowledge graphs concurrently,
principled conflict resolution becomes critical.
Existing RDF CRDT approaches either require dedicated infrastructure or introduce
consistency boundary problems when storing bookkeeping across multiple resources.
We instantiate the state-based OR-Set of Bieniusa et al. over RDF 1.2 triple terms,
keeping all bookkeeping within a single dataset, and demonstrate a proof-of-concept datastore wrapper
for Comunica, enabling agents to query and update a conflict-free knowledge graph via standard SPARQL,
unaware of the underlying merge machinery.
Looking forward, we plan to investigate constraint-aware merge semantics for RDF CRDTs.
