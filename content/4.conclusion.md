## Conclusion
{:#conclusion}

We presented a state-based add-wins set CRDT modelled entirely in RDF 1.2,
requiring no infrastructure beyond what most Web servers already support: RDF-over-HTTP with ETag support.
All bookkeeping stays within the dataset itself, eliminating the consistency boundary problem.
An NTP-based tombstone pruning mechanism keeps the CRDT manageable without requiring a known replica set,
and quad-store-level abstraction makes conflict resolution fully transparent to agents querying via SPARQL.
Looking forward, we plan to overcome this works weakness which is a lack of constraint violation checking -
meaning two non-violating copies always merge into a non-violating state.
