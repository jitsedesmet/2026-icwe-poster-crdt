## Add-wins State-based CRDT-RDF
{:#core}

Let $$ \mathcal{E} $$ be the countably infinit set of _elements_, and let $$ \mathcal{T} $$ be the countably infinite set of _unique tags_.
A state based OR-set $$ S $$ is a subset of $$ \mathcal{E} \times 2^\mathcal{T} \times 2^\mathcal{T} $$,
meaning it is a set of triples $$ (e, addS, removeS) \text{ with } e \in \mathcal{E} \text{, } addS \subset \mathcal{T} \text{and } removeS \subset \mathcal{T} $$.
Each element $$ e $$ can appear in the set only once:
$$\forall (e, addS, removeS ) \in S . \forall (e', addS', removeS') \in S . e = e' \implies addS = addS' \land removeS = removeS' $$.
We call an element $$ e $$ _present the set_ when $$ present(e, S) \Leftrightarrow \exists(e', addS, \_) \in S . e = e' \land addS \ne \emptyset $$.
Three operations exist on the set, namely _merge_ ($$\sqcup$$) (commutative, associative, and idempotent), _add_, and _remove_:

<div markdown="1" style="font-size: 0.9em">
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_1 \sqcup S_2 =& \{ ( e, addS, removeS ) \mid \\
&\kern{2em}(\exists (e_1, addS_1, removeS_1) \in S_1 . \exists (e_2, addS_2, removeS_2) \in S_2 . e = e_1 \land e = e_2 \land \\
&\kern{2em}\quad removeS = removeS_1 \cup removeS_2 \land addS = (addS_1 \cup addS_2) \setminus removeS) \\
&\kern{2em}\lor ( (e, addS, removeS) \in S_1 \land \lnot(\exists addS', removeS' . (e, addS', removeS') \in S_2 ) ) \\
&\kern{2em}\lor ( (e, addS, removeS) \in S_2 \land \lnot(\exists addS', removeS' . (e, addS', removeS') \in S_1 ) ) \\
add(e, S) =& \text{ let } t \in \mathcal{T} \text{ be a fresh unique tag. Then} \\
&\begin{cases}
S \setminus \{(e, addS, removeS)\} \cup \{(e, addS \cup \{t\}, removeS)\} & \text{if } \exists\,(e, addS, removeS) \in S \\
S \cup \{(e, \{t\}, \emptyset)\} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
remove(e, S) =& \\
& \begin{cases}
S \setminus \{(e, addS, removeS)\} \cup \{(e, \emptyset, removeS \cup addS)\} & \text{if } \exists\,(e, addS, removeS) \in S \\
S & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$
</div>

Practically tying the CRDT to RDF means we instantiate $$ \mathcal{E} $$ as all valid RDF 1.2 triples,
and instantiate $$ \mathcal{T} $$ as all valid UUD V4 tags. Within a given RDF 1.2 graph, we then model this information as:

```
:me a :human .
[] crdt:tags <<( :me a :human ) >> ;
   crdt:add "96d482e5-3ce7-4f24-a21b-a9d0506ff5b0"^^crdt:uuidv4 ;
   crdt:remove "77c01067-1594-475e-8c64-76f9c4ec4402"^^crdt:uuidv4 .
```
