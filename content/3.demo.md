## Add-wins State-based CRDT-RDF
{:#core}

A state-based OR-set of a set $$ s $$ containing elements $$ e \in E $$,
is a set containing triples of type $$ s^{or}_{el} : E \times 2^T \times 2^T $$ with $$ T $$ the set of all _unique tags_.
Concretely, the set state based OR-set $$ s^{or} \in 2^{s^{or}_{el}} $$ is a set in the powerset of $$ s^{or}_{el} $$.
Each element $$ e $$ can be present only once:
$$\forall (e, addS, removeS ) \in s^{or} . \forall (e', addS', removeS') \in s^{or} . e = e' \implies addS = addS' \land removeS = removeS' $$.
Element $$ e $$ is present in the represented set $$ s $$ in case: $$ e \in s \Leftrightarrow \exists(e', addS, \_) \in s^{or} . e = e' \land addS \ne \emptyset $$ .
Furthermore, the commutative, associative, and idempotent merge function is defined as:
<div markdown="1" style="font-size: 0.9em">
$$
\begin{aligned}
s_1^{or} \sqcup s_2^{or} = \{ ( &e, addS, removeS ) \in s^{or}_{el} \mid \\&
(\exists (e_1, addS_1, removeS_1) \in s_1^{or} . \exists (e_2, addS_2, removeS_2) \in s_2^{or} . e = e_1 \land e = e_2 \land \\&
    \quad removeS = removeS_1 \cup removeS_2 \land addS = (addS_1 \cup addS_2) \setminus removeS) \\&
\lor ( (e, addS, removeS) \in s_1^{or} \land \lnot(\exists addS', removeS' . (e, addS', removeS') \in s_2^{or} ) ) \\&
\lor ( (e, addS, removeS) \in s_2^{or} \land \lnot(\exists addS', removeS' . (e, addS', removeS') \in s_1^{or} ) )
\end{aligned}
$$
</div>

While the add operation and remove operation are defined as follows:
<div markdown="1" style="font-size: 0.9em">
$$
\begin{aligned}
add(e, s^{or}) = \text{let } t \in T \text{ be a fresh unique tag. Then}
\begin{cases}
    s^{or} \setminus \{(e, addS, removeS)\} \cup \{(e, addS \cup \{t\}, removeS)\} & \text{if } \exists\,(e, addS, removeS) \in s^{or} \\
    s^{or} \cup \{(e, \{t\}, \emptyset)\} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$
</div>

<div markdown="1" style="font-size: 0.9em">
$$
remove(e, s^{or}) = \begin{cases}
s^{or} \setminus \{(e, addS, removeS)\} \cup \{(e, \emptyset, removeS \cup addS)\} & \text{if } \exists\,(e, addS, removeS) \in s^{or} \\
s^{or} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
</div>
