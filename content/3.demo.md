## CRDT-RDF
{:#core}

A state-based OR-set is a set of triples $$ S = \{ (e, addS, removeS), \ldots \} $$,
Each element $$ e $$ is contained only once in the set:
$$\forall (e, addS_1, removeS_1), (e, addS_2, removeS_2) \in s . addS_1 = addS_2 \land removeS_1 = removeS_2  $$,
and the empty set is defined as $$ s^0 = \emptyset $$
An element $$e$$ is present in the modeled set:
$$ e \in query(s) \Leftrightarrow \exists(e, addS, removeS) \in s . addS \ne \emptyset $$


The merge operation is defined as:

$$
\begin{aligned}
s_1 \sqcup s_2 = \{ ( &e, addS, removeS ) \mid \\&
(\exists addS_1, addS_2, removeS_1, removeS_2 . (e, addS_1, removeS_1) \in s_1 \land \\&
    \quad (e, addS_2, removeS_2) \in s_2 \land removeS = removeS_1 \cup removeS_2 \land addS = (addS_1 \cup addS_2) \setminus removeS) \\&
\lor ( (e, addS, removeS) \in s_1 \land \lnot(\exists addS', removeS' . (e, addS', removeS') \in s_2 ) ) \\&
\lor ( (e, addS, removeS) \in s_2 \land \lnot(\exists addS', removeS' . (e, addS', removeS') \in s_1 ) )
\end{aligned}
$$


$$
\begin{aligned}
add(e, s) = \begin{cases}
s \setminus \{(e, addS, removeS)\} \cup \{(e, addS \cup \{t\}, removeS)\} & \exists\,(e, addS, removeS) \in s \\
s \cup \{(e, \{t\}, \emptyset)\} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\quad \text{where } t \text{ is a fresh unique tag}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
remove(e, s) = \begin{cases}
s \setminus \{(e, addS, removeS)\} \cup \{(e, \emptyset, removeS \cup addS)\} & \exists\,(e, addS, removeS) \in s \\
s & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
